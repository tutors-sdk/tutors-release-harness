## Why 2: Why do a fix and the claim written for it both read as unmatched?

Because the matcher never offers an info hunk to a claim. The four claims in the monorepo's `release/claims.yaml`
(Rules 0216, 0051 twice and 0036, added by [#364](https://github.com/tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo/pull/364)) name
exactly the five `axe` hunks, and all five are severity info ("axe violation fixed on b"). `src/claims/matcher.ts`
assigns only failing hunks ("Info hunks are reported but never need a claim"), so the four claims are never used
and `staleClaims` lists them, and `compare.matches` carries no claim for the fixes.
