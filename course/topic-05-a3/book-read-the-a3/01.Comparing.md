# What is compared, and the Gate

Under the title, find the sentence that begins **This sheet compares**. It names:

- the side **b** build: `main` at a short commit sha (a Main to RC forecast), or a release candidate;
- the side **a** release: the production version it was judged against;
- when the run happened.

Next to it is the **Gate**: PASS, WARN or FAIL, with the line under it saying why (for example *every difference is claimed*). If the Gate passed, the **Release Confidence Score** and its band follow, with the eight dimensions. A dimension marked `n/m` was **not measured**: it is left out of the score, never scored 100.

> Check: is the production version the one students use today? If the sheet compares against an older release, every number below it describes the wrong gap.

Write down the verdict, the score and the band.
