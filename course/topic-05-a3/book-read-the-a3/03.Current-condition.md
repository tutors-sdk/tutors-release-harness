# Current condition and the control charts

**The quality strip** sits under the Gate: Speed, Metrics and Tests. Each light is *within reason*, *look* or *not measured* (hatched, never green). Open "What each light read" to see the rule and the reading behind each one.

**Timing on control charts** shows one chart per journey. The shaded band is production's limits; each dot is main's median for a night.

- The centre line is production's mean; the UCL and LCL are centre ± 2.66 × the mean moving range, and the chart prints the figures.
- A dot outside the band is a **special cause to investigate**. It turns Speed to *look*, and never fails anything.

**Fixed on main** lists accessibility violations and console errors that are gone on main. That is good news, shown beside the traffic lights rather than on them.

**Real courses** shows the course corpus loaded in production's reader and main's. A page that is *worse on main* is listed with its reasons.

Note any light at *look*, any dot outside its band, and any course worse on main.
