# Harness 1.22.0 (contract 1.22.0)

The policy family: what b must be. Every engine so far is a diff, so a fault on both sides was at
most informational. The /auth 500 in 16.2.0 failed identically on a and b and was reported as such.
A diff can say the candidate is no worse than production; it cannot say production was acceptable
to begin with. This release adds a second kind of check, judged on the candidate alone, and ships
all of it **informing**. The second step of the "Policy" release. The contract changelog entry is
in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor** for three artefacts and the new capture
and `readiness.json` fields.

## What is new

- **Three checks on b alone** (`src/compare/policy.ts`), each an artefact whose findings are hunks
  beside the diff, under the same Gate and claimable the same way:
  - `image-hardening`: runs as root, declares no `HEALTHCHECK`, or has something that looks like a
    secret in its environment or its layer history. Read from the image config the manifest engine
    already collects, plus `docker image history --no-trunc`. Names and reasons are kept; a value
    never is.
  - `build-provenance`: no SLSA provenance verified under the publishing identity, or one whose
    builder and workflow do not name `image-build.yml`. `cosign verify-attestation` on the digest,
    as for the SBOM, once for SLSA v1 and once for v0.2.
  - `vuln-ceiling`: a critical or high advisory with a fix available, from the scan already run,
    whether or not production has it too.
- **Informing, with no date set.** A finding is reported, never gated: no verdict, Gate or exit
  code changes. 2.0 decides which become blocking, and none does before it has a planted mutant that
  it catches.
- **A fault on both sides is visible.** Each finding ends "(production too)" or "(new on b)".
- **Never silently green.** Each app gets a `summary` hunk saying what each check found to hold, or
  `not-evaluated` saying why it could not run: an image built here, or not signature-verified in
  the run, has no verified attestation to read.
- **Shown at a glance.** `report.html` and `report.md` gain a **Policy** table (one row per app, one
  column per check); the findings are listed under Informing, and the readiness page breaks its
  informing count down by check.

## What it will find on today's images

Read from the monorepo at 865d02f, the commit of the 30 Sep forecast. The runtime stage of its
Dockerfile has no `HEALTHCHECK`, so `image-hardening` finds one per app; `USER 1001` and an
environment of `NODE_ENV`, `PORT`, `HOST`, `NODE_OPTIONS`, `GIT_SHA` and `BUILD_DATE` pass.
`image-build.yml` signs each digest and attests its SBOM, but attests no SLSA provenance, so
`build-provenance` finds one per app, on production too. The ceiling depends on the night's
database. The 16.2.2 images carry a critical `tar` advisory, so expect it to fire. The kept
forecasts keep counts, not findings, so the first forecast on 1.22.0 is the first to say.
