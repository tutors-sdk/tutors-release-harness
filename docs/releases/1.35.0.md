# Harness 1.35.0 (contract 1.35.0)

The harness's own service levels: freshness and rates. The contract changelog entry is in
[../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor**, for a new optional `readiness.json` field.
**No verdict, Gate or exit code moves.**

## Why

1.34.0 holds the candidate to service level objectives, stricter than the monorepo's. The harness
should hold itself to some too. A Main to RC forecast three days old, or one judged on half the
nights, says less than its Gate suggests, and until now nothing on the page said so.

## What is new

- **Freshness.** The newest Main to RC forecast and the newest nightly A/A are each at most 26 hours
  old (a day, plus slack for a slow runner); the newest mutants self-test at most 8 days.
- **Rates**, over the last 14 finished nights: at least 90% judged or unchanged, at least 90% with a
  clean, verified A/A; and 100% of planted mutants caught by the newest self-test.
- **Opinionated, and a ratchet.** `SERVICE_LEVEL_TARGETS` is pinned by
  `tests/service-levels.test.ts`; raise a target by PR, never lower one to turn the panel green.
- `readiness.json` gains `serviceLevels`; `readiness.html` a **service levels** panel under the soak.
  A level whose input was not read is "not judged", never guessed.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Advisory, like the rest of the readiness page.
