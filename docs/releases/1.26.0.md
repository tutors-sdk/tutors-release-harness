# Harness 1.26.0 (contract 1.26.0)

The timing tolerance: runway improvement D, after Kayenta's effect-size tolerance. The contract
changelog entry is in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor**, for a new check and
artefact name. **The verdict never moves**: the check ships informing.

## Why

`timing` calls a page or a journey "slower" when the difference is significant and the median
moved by at least 20% (`timing.minEffect`). A significant 12% slowdown was not reported at all.
Nothing separated "slower" from "slower and it matters".

## What is new

- **`timing-tolerance`** judges timing's own samples a second time: page TTFB, journey duration and
  the k6 leg's p95. A slowdown that is significant and at least **10%** of production's median
  **matters**, and is a finding. One under 10% is within tolerance. Each finding says whether
  `timing` fails it too (20% or more) or only the tolerance reports it, and the smallest slowdown
  the samples could have detected.
- **Informing, with no date.** `ENGINE_LEVELS` lists it beside the policy family. Its findings
  are labelled informing and never gate. At 2.0 it becomes blocking, and a significant slowdown of
  10% or more fails a release, where today it takes 20%.
- **A planted mutant**, `slow-ssr-mild`, adds 150 ms to every HTML response and must be
  attributed to `timing-tolerance`: fourteen mutants.
- **The soak watches it.** The readiness page's soak lists every informing check (`SOAK_CHECKS`),
  each with its quiet nights and its mutant. Until now it listed only the policy family.
