# Harness 1.48.4 (contract 1.48.4)

The keyboard-order walk starts at the top of the page. A **patch**.

## Why

Since 17 Oct 2026 the post-deploy monitor (issue #131) has been red on one difference: on `reference:course`,
production's keyboard order began at "My courses" and the course's topic cards, where the recorded 17.0.0-rc.1 began
at "Skip to content" and the header. Production serves that same commit, so the page is the same; the walk was not.

`focusWalk` blurred the focused element and pressed Tab. A blur does not move the browser's starting point for Tab:
it stays where focus last was. A page that had put focus into its content before the walk was walked from there, and
the walk said "keyboard order changed" about a page whose order had not changed. A two-line page shows it: with focus
last on the second link, blur then Tab lands on the third, not the first.

## What changed

- The walk puts a mark (`span#tutors-harness-focus-start`, `tabindex=-1`) at the top of the body, focuses it, walks,
  and removes it. The first Tab lands on the page's first stop, whatever had focus before.
- A page already walked from the top gives the same stops as before, so the recorded releases and the A/A history
  stay comparable. A page that was not now gives its header first.

## What to expect

The next post-deploy run either goes green (the walk was the cause) or still shows a `focus` difference on
`reference:course` that now starts at the top on both sides, which would then be a real one about production.
