# Harness 1.43.0 (contract 1.43.0)

Rule journeys. The harness drives a student's side of Tutors as EARS Rules written in Gherkin, compares it on both
sides like every other journey, and says which Rules a difference is about. A **minor**: a new journey set, a new
optional report field.

## Why

Until now the harness compared a course: anonymous pages, one signed-in topic. Leigh (17 Oct 2026) asked for journeys a
layer deeper for students and lecturers, written the BDD and EARS way and grounded on the monorepo's current main, so a
difference reads as a behaviour that moved. The proposal is the Claude Doc "Harness user journeys" (Rules 0582 to 0592);
this release lands its first two.

## What changed

- **The `rules` set.** `traffic/journeys/features/student.feature` holds Rule 0582 (a signed-in student reads a lab and
  moves between its steps; exercises 0011, 0250, 0252, 0260, 0303, 0330) and Rule 0583 (a student picks a course
  sentiment and sees themself in the online list; exercises 0046, 0064, 0280). A small Gherkin reader
  (`traffic/journeys/gherkin.ts`) and a step list (`traffic/journeys/steps.ts`) turn each Rule into a journey; a
  sentence no step matches fails when the journeys load, with its file and line. `@role-<role>` picks who the identity
  stub signs in as. `harness journeys` prints each Rule journey's Rule and what it exercises.
- **Attribution.** `report.json` `causes.rules`, `report.md`, `report.html` and the A3 gap section list the Rule
  journeys whose pages or ledgers moved, with the Rules they exercise. Informational.
- **Sign-in pacing.** The reader limits sign-in paths to 3 in 10 seconds and resets only after a quiet gap; the
  harness waits out the gap per reader origin after two quick sign-ins.
- **The local edge sends `X-Forwarded-For`**, as production's does, so the signed-in reader no longer logs "could not
  determine a client IP" on one side and not the other.

## Proof

A local A/A of main's images (`--runs 3 --set rules,auth`) is PASS and clean.

## What a consumer does

Nothing. A report has more pages (the Rule journeys'), and a claim names one by its page key. `--set rules` runs only
them; `--set fixture,auth,reference,replay` runs what 1.42 ran.
