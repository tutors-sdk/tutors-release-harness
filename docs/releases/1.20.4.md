# Harness 1.20.4 (contract 1.20.4)

Orphan diffs measured on a forecast. Every kept forecast's `changes.json` said orphan diffs were
"not measured": CHANGELOG.md at a commit of main has no `### v<version>` section, and no
`--changelog` was given, although `harness changes` has taken one since 1.10.0 and the monorepo
has had `pnpm release:changelog --json` to give it. The fifth step of the "Explain" release. The
contract changelog entry is in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **patch**: no command,
flag, `report.json`, `changes.json` or index field, verdict, Gate or exit code changes.

## What is new

- **Main to RC gives `harness changes` a changelog.** The publish job's "What changed between
  production and main" step runs `pnpm release:changelog --from v<production> --to <commit> --json`
  in the monorepo checkout it already has (full history, which the release job's Rules checkout,
  shallow, does not), in a step of its own that holds no token, since it runs the monorepo's
  code, and passes it as `--changelog`.
- **Orphan diffs are measured**: a PR that changed a product section's paths that CHANGELOG.md does
  not name. Over the 30 Sep forecast's range (v16.2.2 to 865d02f, 69 PRs) the changelog has 69
  entries, 8 already named in CHANGELOG.md, and `harness changes` measures 39 orphan diffs where
  it said "not measured". Change risk was already 0 and floored on that forecast, so its score does
  not move; the 39 are now listed with their PRs.
- **Orphan entries stay not measured** on a forecast: the tooling's changelog is derived from the
  diff, so it cannot name an entry with no diff.
- **Best effort.** When the changelog cannot be built (install, history, its step's 5 minutes) or
  is not version 1, the step says so and `harness changes` runs without it, as before.
