The smallest detectable slowdown. The 27 Sep forecast said "journey anonymous-student-searches

## What is new

- **The effect and the smallest detectable slowdown beside each timing verdict.** A `timing` hunk
  the Mann-Whitney test judged (page TTFB, journey duration, k6 load) ends with
  `smallest slowdown 5/5 runs could detect: about 25%`; a not significant one also says its shift
  and sample sizes: `median 4200ms → 5100ms but not significant (p=0.095, +21%, n=5/5; smallest
  slowdown 5/5 runs could detect: about 25%)`. A journey counts runs; a page's TTFB counts its visits
  and load its k6 samples, so those say `n/n samples`. Read +21% beside 25% as "these runs could not
  tell", not as "no change".
- **How it is found** (`smallestDetectableSlowdown` in `src/compare/stats.ts`): b's samples are
  scaled to a's median, keeping b's spread, and slowed uniformly in 0.5% steps until the same test
  says p < alpha. A binary search over the steps keeps it to a dozen tests, even for k6's
  thousands of samples.
- **Reported, never judged.** It is not a tolerance: no verdict, Gate or exit code reads it, and
  the first `p=` in a summary is still the test's, the one confidence and the glance read.
- The kept forecasts do not keep their samples, so their figures cannot be recomputed; the next
  forecast is the first to carry them.
