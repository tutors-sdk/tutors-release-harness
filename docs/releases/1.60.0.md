# Harness 1.60.0 (contract 1.60.0)

The harness reads the logs of the signed-in time and live apps, and an app that wrote nothing on one side is one difference. This is a **minor**: `capture.json` `logs` gains two apps and `logs` hunks gain the `<app>/silent` scope.

## Why

The 29 Oct forecast (`sha-011b8c6` beside 17.0.0, harness 1.59.1) reported 17 `logs` differences on `time`: every field "gone on b" and request-id propagation "fell from 100% to 0%". The time app's logging was not broken. Since mono-repo #327 the time journeys (Rules 0590 and 0591) open the time app only signed in, on `time-auth-*`, and the harness never read that service's logs. The anonymous `time-*` service now sees only the harness's own `/healthz` and `/metrics`, which the request logger skips when they succeed (mono-repo #515). 17.0.0 logged each `/metrics` scrape, which is where side a's lines came from.

## What changed

- `src/stack.ts` `LOG_APPS`: a compose capture reads the logs of `reader`, `catalogue`, `live`, `time`, `reader-auth`, `live-auth` and `time-auth`. The time app's real request lines are now compared, under `time-auth/<field or level>`.
- `src/compare/engines.ts` `logs`: when one side wrote no line at all and the other wrote JSON lines, the engine reports one hunk, `<app>/silent` ("wrote no log line on b (N on a): nothing reached it, or it stopped logging"), instead of one per field, per level and the request-id ratio. Lines that are written but are not JSON are still `<app>/json`.

## Expect

The next forecast shows `time/silent` in place of the 17 `time` hunks, and `time-auth` and `live-auth` log hunks wherever the signed-in apps' logs differ between the two sides.
