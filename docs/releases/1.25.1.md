# Harness 1.25.1 (contract 1.25.1)

Claims with a lifetime. A claim said "this difference is intended" and then said it for ever: one
written for 16.3.0 would quietly cover the same scope in 16.4.0. This is runway improvement E, after
Conforma's `effectiveUntil` and `imageRef`. The contract changelog entry is in
[../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **patch** for two optional claim fields and one optional
report field. **The verdict never moves**: lifetimes ship informing.

## What is new

- **`until`** on a claim: the last UTC day (`"2026-11-30"`) or release (`"16.3.0"`, `v` allowed) the
  claim is meant for. A date expires after that day. A release expires when the candidate is a later
  release (its own release candidates are not later), or, on a forecast whose candidate is
  `sha-<short>`, once production has reached that release.
- **`digests`** on a claim: the image each named app had when the claim was written,
  `reader: "sha256:<64 hex>"` and so on. It expires when the candidate's image for a named app is
  another, or has no registry digest.
- **Reported, still covering.** `report.json` gains `claimLifetimes`: every claim with a lifetime,
  live or expired, why, and the failing differences it covers. `report.html` and `report.md` say
  "N of M claim(s) with a lifetime have expired; they still cover K failing difference(s), which
  would be unclaimed once claim lifetimes block (2.0)" beside the A/A lines.
- **The 2.0 switch is one line.** `CLAIM_LIFETIME_LEVEL` in `src/claims/lifetime.ts` is
  `informing`. At 2.0 it becomes `blocking`: an expired claim is left out before matching, so what it
  covered is unclaimed and the claim is stale.
- A bad `until` (not a real day or an `X.Y.Z`), an app other than the four, or a digest that is not
  `sha256:` and 64 hex characters is an invalid claims file, exit `2`, naming the claim and the field.

## In the monorepo

`pnpm check:release-claims` accepts `until`, `digests` and the policy-check artefacts
(`image-hardening`, `build-provenance`, `vuln-ceiling`) in tutors-mono-repo, Rules 0230 to 0233.
Until that is merged, a release branch whose claims use the new fields fails the monorepo's
pre-check (which refuses unknown fields) even though the harness accepts them.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Add `until: "<the release>"` to a claim written for one release so it cannot cover
the next one; add `digests` (from the forecast's or candidate's `provenance.b.images`, or the
readiness page) to a claim about one exact build.
