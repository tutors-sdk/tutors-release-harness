# Harness 1.39.0 (contract 1.39.0)

Timing on control charts. A **minor**: new optional fields, `timingControl` in `report.json` and `timing` in
`a3.json`. Advice only: no verdict, Gate or exit code moves.

## Why

Timing stopped failing releases in 1.38.0. Leigh (15 Oct 2026): it should instead be flagged when it is outside
known control charts' upper and lower bounds, the Lean way to show outliers for investigation rather than an
outright fail.

## What changed

- **In the run** (`src/compare/timing-control.ts`): each journey's durations on a are an XmR chart (centre ± 2.66 ×
  the mean moving range, provisional under 10 points); b's runs above the UCL or below the LCL are a special cause.
  `report.json` records it as `timingControl`, and a timing hunk's "what to check" opens with the reading.
- **On the A3** (`src/a3/timing.ts`): "Timing on control charts", one small chart per journey: production's limits
  as a band, main's median night by night as dots, red outside the band. The limits come from production's nightly
  medians beside the same production once there are two nights, else from production's runs in the newest
  forecast. The Speed light says "look" when a journey is outside them, even if no test called it slower.
