# Harness 1.12.0 (contract 1.12.0)

The reviewer's glance (C3): gemba, go to the artefact and look. 1.9.0 gave every release a score,
1.10.0 said which PR each point came from, and 1.11.0 kept them over time. A score still asks a
person to trust a number; the glance hands them the places to look instead. At the top of
`report.html`, `report.md` and the PR comment, right under the Gate, the RCS and its band, are
at most seven ranked findings, each linked to the hunk, the claim that covers it and the PR that
caused it, and a Reviewer records one mark per item. Nothing in it is a break: a consumer
written against 1.11.0 keeps working, and no verdict or exit code changes. It is a **minor**
release because it adds a command, four flags, a file and fields. The contract changelog entry
is in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes); the Lean view behind it is
[docs/lean.md](../lean.md); the standard work around it is `release/SOP.md` in the monorepo
(step 8 is the glance, step 9 the go / no-go it feeds).

## What is new

- **The glance in `confidence.json`**: `glance` (at most seven items: `rank`, `kind`, `key`,
  `finding`, `links` with `hunk`, `claim`, `pr` and `diff`, `novelty`, `exposure`, `score`,
  `basis`, `mark`) and `glanceBasis` (the rule, the releases novelty read, the journey set,
  the candidates per kind, what was not checked and why). `harness confidence` and the score
  stage of `harness release` rank it from the same inputs as the score; the score never reads it.
- **At the top of the report.** `report.md`, `gate.md` (the PR comment) and `report.html` show
  the glance right after the RCS line, with one line on how to mark and, on an Amber release,
  that the glance must be recorded verified before go. The terminal prints one line for it.
- **`harness glance mark --run <dir> --item <n> --mark verified|disputed|escalated --by <name>
  [--note text]`** appends the mark to `glance-marks.jsonl` beside `confidence.json`
  ([schema](../contract/glance-marks.schema.json)) and re-renders the glance where it is shown.
- **`harness glance status --run <dir> [--json]`** prints the glance with its marks and whether
  an Amber release's glance is recorded verified. It exits `0` whatever it finds.
- **On the scoreboard**, two optional fields: `maskIds` (so the next release can see a mask
  added) and `glance` (items, marks, the kinds checked and every candidate's key, which the next
  release's novelty reads). Older lines stay valid.
- **`harness confidence --scoreboard <file>`** names the history (default
  `HARNESS_HOME/scoreboard/releases.jsonl`); `release.yml`'s `scoreboard` job now scores after it
  fetches the `scoreboard` branch and passes it.

## Where to look, and in what order

| Kind | A place to look is | From |
| --- | --- | --- |
| broad claim | a claim broad enough to absorb what nobody named, with its `approvedBy` and every hunk it absorbed | the release report |
| mask added | a new blind spot since the last release, its reason and how much it hid | `masksApplied` against the last scoreboard line's `maskIds` |
| near miss | a timing or load p-value in 0.05-0.10, both distributions side by side | the release report, the captures, k6 |
| hotspot, first contribution | a file changed in three of the last six releases, touched by someone's first PR, with the diff | `changes.json` |
| new persistence write | a journey that wrote nothing and now writes, even when claimed | the captures |
| fixed on b | a console error or axe violation gone on b; with new errors on the same page it asks "fixed, or only changed?" | the release report |
| major bump | a major dependency bump and the journeys exercising its app, or "unmapped" | `changes.json` |
| duration moved | a journey whose median duration moved more than 20%, significant or not | the captures |

**Ranked by novelty × exposure**, highest first, the order above breaking ties. Novelty is
(n − seen + 1) ÷ (n + 1) over the last six releases on the scoreboard that recorded that kind:
1 with no history (the item says "no history yet"), lower each time the same finding came back,
never 0. Exposure is the share of the journey set the finding touches; a finding the harness
cannot place on a journey counts as the whole set and says "unmapped", so an unknown is not
ranked below a known. Both factors are written out on every item, and the whole ranking is in
`confidence.json`, so the glance can itself be reviewed: when a regression escapes past a
verified glance, the 5 Whys asks whether it ranked the right thing.

## Honest about what it cannot see

A kind whose input the harness does not have is listed under the glance as **not checked**, with
the reason. It is never scored, never guessed. The checked-in 16.2.1 → 16.2.2 run, scored from
its `report.json` alone, checks three kinds (broad claims, near misses, fixes) and lists five as
not checked: masks added (no scoreboard), hotspots and major bumps (no `changes.json`),
persistence writes and duration moves (no `capture.json`). Its glance ranks three fixes; the
first is the catalogue's `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'from')`, which reads as
gone on b only because its stack changed, and the glance asks "fixed, or only changed?". A full
`harness release` run with the monorepo checkout has six kinds live; masks added becomes live
from the second release on, once a scoreboard line carries `maskIds`.

## The marks

| Mark | Means | Becomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| verified | looked, and agrees with the claim | counts towards go |
| disputed | looked, and does not agree | a new claim or a hold |
| escalated | cannot tell from the artefacts | a 5 Whys on why the harness could not show it (`whyWanted: true`, the seam for `harness why`, C4) |

`glance-marks.jsonl` is append-only: a changed mind is a new line, and the latest line for a
finding is its mark. A mark names its finding by kind and key as well as rank, so a re-score that
reorders the glance keeps each mark on its finding. When `--by` is named as a PR's author in
`changes.json`, the command says so (the SOP's Reviewer authored no PR in the release) and
records the mark all the same: the deviation is the Captain's to log.

## Guardrails, in code

- **The Gate wins.** The glance is ranked after the exit code is decided and never read by the
  score or the gate. A mark rewrites the glance block and the `mark` fields and nothing else.
  Tests hold that `harness release` exits the same with a glance, without one, and after every
  mark, and that `status.json`'s `exitCode` does not move.
- **Seven, and no more.** The cap is the plan's "where to stop"; an eighth kind is added only when
  a 5 Whys shows an escape the eight could not surface.
- **Contributor lines are for trends and glances, never people's reviews.** A first contribution
  is a fact about a PR meeting a hotspot; the finding names the PR and the file, never the person.
- **A glance that cannot be built** is an empty glance with every kind not checked and the
  reason; it never stops the score.

## Behaviour that changes without a flag or field

- `harness release` prints a `glance:` line after the scoreboard lines, and the score stage's
  note says how many items there are to mark. Its plan names the glance in the score stage.
- `report.md`, `gate.md` and `report.html` of `harness release` have the glance under the RCS
  line, between `<!-- glance:start -->` and `<!-- glance:end -->`; the "Glance (gemba)" section
  lower down points to it instead of saying "not built yet".
- The board `harness confidence` prints lists the glance after the deductions.
- `harness confidence` reads `HARNESS_HOME/scoreboard/releases.jsonl` for novelty when it exists.
