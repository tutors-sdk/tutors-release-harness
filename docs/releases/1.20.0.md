# Harness 1.20.0 (contract 1.20.0)

Causes, not hunks. The forecast of 29 September listed 899 unclaimed differences, and 696 of them
were one Dockerfile change seen once per package per image. Every report now folds its unclaimed
differences into the causes behind them and leads its differences with them: "899 unclaimed
differences, 19 causes". The first step of the "Explain" release. The contract changelog entry is
in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor** release, because `report.json` gains an
optional field: no command, flag, verdict, Gate or exit code changes, and nothing that judges reads
the causes.

## What is new

- **`causes` in `report.json`**, computed from `compare.unclaimed` after the verdict, when the
  report is written (`src/report/causes.ts`, under `src/report/` so nothing that gates reads it).
  A cause is an artefact and a kind: the summary with the app or page taken off, the name it ends
  in (a package, a log field, a header) folded into `items`, hashed build assets folded into one,
  and every number masked. A network request keeps its route unless it is a hashed build asset.
  Each cause has its hunk count, the apps and pages it spans, what it folded and one example.
- **Moved together**: the pages on which two or more artefacts moved, grouped by the same set of
  artefacts. The Paper rebuild reads as `dom`, `focus`, `headers`, `network`, `screenshot` on six
  pages, not as five unrelated counts.
- **The cause table** leads the differences in `report.md` and `report.html`, each example linked to
  its row.
- On the five kept forecasts: 865 unclaimed differences fold into 19 causes (27 September), 892
  into 19 and 893 into 19 (28 September), 899 into 19 (29 September), and 204 into 19 on
  30 September, after the sbom claim took the 696 "package removed" hunks, which were one cause.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. A report written before 1.20.0 has no `causes`; `harness compare` on its captures
writes one that does.
