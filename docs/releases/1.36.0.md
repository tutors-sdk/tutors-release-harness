# Harness 1.36.0 (contract 1.36.0)

The reader's Supabase Realtime cost per page view, side a against side b. The contract changelog
entry is in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor**, for a new check and artefact
name. **The verdict never moves**: the check ships informing.

## Why

On 13 Oct 2026 the project's Realtime quota was overrun. The reader joined a Realtime channel per
course and broadcast every page view to every classmate, so the messages one class cost grew with
the square of the class. tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo#474 makes readers publish-only: a page view is
one HTTP POST to `/realtime/v1/api/broadcast`, and only Tutors Live dashboards and shared
whiteboards open a websocket. Nothing in the harness could see that change or its regression: the
`persistence-stub-requests` mask drops every `/realtime/v1/` request from `network`, and no
collector watched websockets.

## What is new

- **The collector** counts, per page, `realtime: { broadcasts, sockets, framesSent }`
  (`src/collectors/realtime.ts`): POSTs to `/realtime/v1/api/broadcast`, websockets opened to
  `/realtime/v1/websocket`, and the data frames sent on them. Phoenix control frames (`phx_join`,
  `phx_leave`, `heartbeat`, `access_token`) are not counted: they are upkeep on timers, and the join
  is what `sockets` reports. The counts sit outside `network`, so the mask does not erase them.
- **`realtime`** (`src/compare/realtime.ts`) judges reader pages (`reader:`, `reader-auth:`,
  `reference:`) with two rules:
  - `<page key>/websocket`, absolute: a reader page opened a Realtime websocket on b. Readers must be
    publish-only. Ends "(production too)" or "(new on b)", as the policy family's findings do.
  - `<app>/messages`, regression: b's Realtime messages per reader page view (broadcasts plus data
    frames, summed over the pages both sides reached in every run) are more than a's, reported as
    "a N / b M per reader page". Fewer is an info hunk.
  There is no threshold, so there is no mask to review: one more message across a run is the
  regression.
- **Informing, with no date.** `ENGINE_LEVELS` lists it; the soak watches it. It has no planted
  mutant yet, so it is not eligible to block.
- A capture recorded before 1.36.0 has no counts. When only one side has them, one info `collection`
  hunk says no comparison was possible.

## In the monorepo

`pnpm check:release-claims` keeps its own copy of the artefact list in
`scripts/checks/release-claims.ts`, and `release/README.md` lists the names. Both must gain
`realtime`, or a claim naming it is rejected before the harness sees it. The behaviour judged is
tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo#474.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. New runs gain informing `realtime` results; no verdict moves. A side a that still
joins course channels is not a finding (only b is judged by the socket rule); a b that does is.
