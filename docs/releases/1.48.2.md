# Harness 1.48.2 (contract 1.48.2)

The post-deploy monitor names the one known difference and stops hiding a warn. A **patch**.

## Why

From 17 Oct 2026 11:58 the post-deploy monitor failed on every run (eight by 18 Oct), with one rollback issue, #131,
whose signature never changed: 24 network differences, `cache-control changed: immutable,max-age=31536000,public → ∅`
on SvelteKit's hashed assets, and one focus difference on `reference:course`. The network differences are known:
Netlify does not send the header the release images do, because the apps' `netlify.toml` sets it on `/build/*` and
SvelteKit serves `/_app/immutable/*` (the monorepo's #500 fixes it; production changes only with a release).

The same differences were there from 15 Oct, yet the monitor was green from 16 Oct 09:48 to 17 Oct 07:46. The A/A of
16 Oct had one difference, so post-deploy could not trust it and gave a warn, advisory only, which exits 0. Nothing on
the run said so.

## What changed

- **`post-deploy/claims.yaml`** claims `network` `GET /_app/immutable/**` for issue #131, `until: "17.0.1"`: it covers
  the 17.0.0 record and expires once the recorded release is 17.0.1 or later. The focus difference on
  `reference:course` is not claimed: production's keyboard walk starts at the course cards, not the header, and the
  missing header does not explain that. It stays news until someone explains or fixes it.
- **`post-deploy.yml`**: a step after the run reads the report's verdict, and on a warn writes a warning annotation
  (`Post-deploy advisory only`) with its reasons and a section in the run's summary.
