# Harness 1.47.0 (contract 1.47.0)

The time app with students in it. Rules 0590 and 0591 have the lecturer open the time app's calendars, medians, lab
views and one student's page, computed from the same seeded rows on both sides. A **minor**: two new host ports and
new pages.

## Why

Until now the harness only opened the time app empty (`time-loads`): in compose it runs in anonymous mode and reads
nothing. The time app is where a lecturer reads who worked how long and on what, and its totals and medians are
computed in the browser from stored rows. A change to that arithmetic, to how labs are grouped or to how students are
ranked moved no artefact the harness compared.

## What changed

- **Rule 0590** (exercises 0319; the monorepo's analytics-calendar feature): with two students' calendar days seeded,
  the lecturer opens the time app for the Rule journeys' course, enters its PIN, and reads the medians (row
  `Median 130 78 104`), the calendar by week and the calendar by day. Three pages: `time-auth:rule-0590-medians`,
  `-calendar-byweek`, `-calendar-byday`.
- **Rule 0591** (exercises 0319; analytics-lab and lab-analytics): with three students' lab records seeded, the lecturer
  reads labs by lab, labs by step, the learning records and the least engaged student's page. Four pages:
  `time-auth:rule-0591-lab-bylab`, `-lab-bystep`, `-learning-records`, `-student`.
- **`time-auth-a` and `time-auth-b`**: a second time app on each compose side, with anonymous mode off and the side's
  persistence stub as its Supabase, on host ports 3106 and 3206 (`TIME_AUTH_PORT_A`/`_B`, so 24 host ports in all).
  `StackUrls.timeAuth` is optional; kind runs no `time-auth`, and there 0590 and 0591 fail with a step that says so.
- **The course's PIN.** The time app reads it from `https://<course id>.netlify.app/tutors.json`. The time journeys name
  the course `harness-rules`, and the lecturer's browser answers that address with the course server's `tutors.json`,
  whose `ignorepin` is now 4321.
- **Seeds.** `POST /_harness/seed` on the persistence stub takes rows by table; the step
  `the persistence stub holds the seed "time-course"` posts `fixtures/persistence/seeds/time-course.json`. Seeded tables
  are kept until the next reset and answer `eq`, `in`, `gte`, `order` and `limit`. Seeded rows are not writes and never
  enter the ledger.
- **Storage keys.** The time app keeps its session under `sb-persistence-a-...` on one side and `sb-persistence-b-...`
  on the other; captures now write `sb-{{persistence}}-`, so the two sides compare.
- New steps: `the lecturer opens the time app at "<view>"`, `the lecturer types "<text>" in the textbox "<name>"`,
  `the page scrolls to the heading "<name>"`. `the page shows no ...` now waits up to the visible timeout for what it
  names to go, so a dialog that is closing is not counted.

## Found on the way (the monorepo's main, `83d4ccf`)

- The time app makes a new Supabase client for every read, and about one first load in eight never finishes loading
  the course (no request open: the clients share one stored session). The step that opens the time app reloads once,
  as a person would; a second stall fails the journey. The fix is mono-repo #507 (Rule 0601), which reuses one client.
- The student page draws its heat maps after load, and their day grids lay out differently from one load to the next
  on the same build. 0591 captures the student's tables, below them.
- Labs by lab and by step key a lab by its last path segment, so two topics that both hold `book-lab-01` add up into one
  column (the enrolled student's 30 minutes are 25 from Topic 1 and 5 from Topic 2).
- The PIN is read from `<id>.netlify.app` only, so a course served from anywhere else can never be opened past the PIN.

## Proved

Run locally on images built from tutors-mono-repo main (`83d4ccf`): `--journey rule-0590-time-calendar --journey
rule-0591-time-labs`, A/A runs with `--runs 3` and `--runs 5`, are clean.

## What a consumer does

Nothing. A local stack publishes two more host ports, and `--port-offset` moves them with the rest.
