# Harness 1.27.0 (contract 1.27.0)

Asset-graph folding: runway change 9. The contract changelog entry is in
[../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor**, for a new check and artefact name. **The
verdict never moves**: the check ships informing, and `network` and `headers` are unchanged.

## Why

On the 2026-10-01 Main to RC forecast, 79 of the 85 `network` hunks were hashed CSS and JS chunks
requested more or fewer times, and all 8 `headers` hunks were the `link` preloads that go with
them. It was one change to how the apps are chunked, and it repeated on every page. Each hunk
needed its own claim.

## What is new

- **`asset-graph`**: one hunk per app whose immutable assets moved. It gives the requests under
  `/_app/immutable/` a to b (JS and CSS), their bytes when every response sent a `content-length`,
  and how many `network` and `headers` differences are this churn. A request answering with another
  status, type, cache header or schema is never churn, and neither is anything outside
  `/_app/immutable/`.
- **Informing, with no date.** The hunk is reported and never gates. `network` and `headers` stay as
  they were. At 2.0 the check becomes blocking: each churn hunk then becomes information ending
  "(folded into asset-graph)", and one claim on `<app>` covers a re-chunking.
- **A planted mutant**, `extra-chunk`: every page loads one more module under `/_app/immutable/`
  (the wrapper serves it). It must be attributed to `asset-graph`: fifteen mutants.
- The capture's network entries gain `bytes`, the response's `content-length` when it sent one.
  No engine diffs it.

Run against the kept 2026-10-01 forecast's hunks, the churn rules pick out the same 79 network and
8 headers hunks. The 6 they leave are `/logo.svg`, `/global.css` and a course image.
