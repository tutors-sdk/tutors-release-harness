# Harness 1.48.6 (contract 1.48.6)

The keyboard-order walk waits for the page to settle after the blur. A **patch**.

## Why

The A/A night of 19 Oct 2026 (01:53) had one difference, so the 19 Oct forecast was only advisory (WARN instead of a verdict): `reader-auth:rule-0583-profile-menu` was walked with the profile menu still open on one side and closed on the other. The same morning the mutants' own A/A on the base was not clean, so no mutant ran on #144, #145 or main. Since 1.48.4 the walk blurs whatever has focus and starts from a mark at the top; a menu that closes when focus leaves it closes on its own clock, and the first Tab raced it.

## What changed

- `src/collectors/browser.ts`: after the blur and before the first Tab, the walk waits until three readings 100 ms apart agree on the visible tabbable elements and no finite animation is running, for at most 2 s.
- A page that was already still is walked exactly as before, so no recorded release needs re-recording.
