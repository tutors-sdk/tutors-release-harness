# Harness 1.38.0 (contract 1.38.0)

Timing is advice, the A3 says why the Gate says what it says, and the last of the sign-in wait is gone. A
**minor**: a new optional `report.json` hunk field, `advice`, and a new optional `a3.json` field, `score.why`.

## Why

The 15 Oct 2026 Main to RC forecast (harness 1.37.4) read FAIL on one difference, "journey student-signs-in
slower on b", 3.4 s against 8.5 s. The sheet said FAIL without saying why, a slowdown gave no hint of what to
look at, and "Console errors 0 of 3 decided" read like three failures when it counts three errors production
shows and main does not.

The 5 s was the harness again. Timing each capture phase on main's image (diagnostic run of 15 Oct 2026) showed
the course and topic pages spending 2.4 s reading network entries and nothing else: the JSON body of
`POST /api/live/activity` (202, a `keepalive` fetch) never arrives in headless Chromium, and the harness waited
its full 3 s for it on every signed-in reader page.

## What changed

- **A write's body is read straight after its status, or not at all.** `bodyReadMs(method)`
  (`src/collectors/browser.ts`): 300 ms for anything but GET and HEAD, 3 s for a read. An unread body is recorded
  as `unread` and, as before, never compared.
- **Timing is informing** (`ENGINE_LEVELS`, `src/compare/levels.ts`), with no date to block: a journey, page or
  load slowdown is reported and never fails a release. `load/errors` (failed or 5xx requests under load) is a
  failure, not timing, and still blocks (`blockingScopes`). The soak does not count timing: it is advice by
  choice, not a check on its way to blocking.
- **A slowdown says what to check** (`advice` on the hunk, `src/compare/timing-advice.ts`), read from the two
  captures: the requests b makes in the journey that a does not, the pages whose first byte moved most (or that
  none did, so the time is in the browser), the command to rerun it alone, and to try it by hand. The report shows
  it under the hunk as "what to check"; the A3's Speed light says "look", "advice, not a failure", with the steps.
- **The Gate says why** (`gateWhy`, `src/a3/model.ts`): a FAIL names each difference with no claim and what turns
  it round; a WARN or PASS gives the run's own first reason. Drawn under the Gate tile.
- **"Fixed on main"** replaces "Decisions on b": "3 console errors production shows and main does not, none
  explained by a claim yet. These count for main, not against it".

A run judged by 1.37.x and one by 1.38.0 are not comparable on timing.
