# Harness 1.51.0 (contract 1.51.0)

The clock probe is wired. A **minor**. Part of the harness hardening plan (production-readiness additions, tranche 1, item E2).

## Why

`src/clock-probe.ts` was written and tested in 1.5.0 and never called. When an app stamps the server's wall clock on a page, the nightly A/A or a release run shows a `dom` hunk on that page and nothing says why; the first reader to see it writes a mask, or blames a PR. The probe can say why from what is already captured: the browser's clock is frozen at HARNESS_NOW, so an instant near the real time of the capture was stamped by the server.

## What changed

- `src/run.ts`: every comparison except post-deploy runs `probeClock` on both raw captures (`clockReading`).
- `report.json` gains `clock: { a, b }`, each `{ verdict, samples }` with up to five instants (`report.schema.json`, `definitions.clockReading`).
- A side whose verdict is `ignores` adds one line to `reasons`: which instant, where, and the frozen instant it should have been.
- Each `dom` hunk on the page where a wall-clock instant was seen, whose lines hold that instant, gains a line of `advice` saying the app ignores HARNESS_NOW and what to do (read `now()` from the shared helper, or mask the stamp with that reason).
- `no-evidence`, `honours` and `indistinguishable` add nothing a reader sees beyond `clock`.
- Post-deploy is skipped: its live side is production, which never has HARNESS_NOW (docs/harness-now.md, point 5).

## Level

Report only. The hunk the advice lands on still fails and is still unclaimed; no verdict, Gate, score or exit code moves.
