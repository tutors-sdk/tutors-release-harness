# Harness 1.18.0 (contract 1.18.0)

The quality strip. Nothing on the A3 said whether tests, speed and metrics were within reason,
and Test signal (15 of the 100 weight points) was "not measured" on every forecast. Three
lights now sit under the Gate on the A3, and the same three as marks on every row of the
overnight readiness page: **Speed**, **Metrics**, **Tests**, each **within reason**, **look** or
**not measured**, with the number, the rule and a link to what it read. The fourth and last
step of the "Page" release. The contract changelog entry is in
[../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor** release, because `harness confidence`,
`harness a3` and `harness readiness` take `--mutants`: no verdict, Gate, exit code or
`report.json` field changes, and the strip is never an input to any of them.

## What is new

- **Speed**: no failing timing, load or startup hunk, and no failed or 5xx k6 request on main.
  Main is measured beside production in the same run, so "within reason" means "not worse than
  production, measured together". On the 30 September forecast: p95 2.19 ms on production,
  2.06 ms on main; 0 of 600 requests failed; no journey significantly slower.
- **Metrics**: no unclaimed metrics hunk. The 15 unclaimed log differences are shown beside it,
  not counted.
- **Tests**, in two halves, each said apart so a half-measured light says which half. The
  harness's: every journey completed on both sides, the A/A the run relied on clean and at most
  2 days old, and the newest weekly mutants self-test caught every mutant. The monorepo's, from
  its quality record (tutors-mono-repo#382): its Nightly green on the commit, and no changed
  package below 80% mutation score. The record is for the judged commit or the newest one
  before it; the light says which, and how old. On 27 September the light reads **look**: the
  `reference-course-reads` journey completed on production and failed on main.
- **Not measured is its own state**, hatched and never green: a light is within reason only
  when every check under it was read and in reason.
- **Test signal reads the monorepo's quality record.** Main to RC fetches `quality/<sha>.json`
  from the monorepo's `quality` branch for the judged commit, or the newest first-parent commit
  before it that has one, keeps it beside the forecast as `quality.json`, and passes it to
  `harness confidence --test-signal`. The record never carries the harness's half:
  `--mutants <mutants.jsonl>` joins the newest weekly self-test as `harnessMutants`, and only
  beside the monorepo's packages, so the harness's own mutants never measure the dimension alone.
  A record with no `packages` (the Nightly wrote no Stryker report) is not measured, never
  exit 2.
- **`quality-strip.schema.json`** describes `quality` in `a3.json` and on each forecast of
  `readiness.json`.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. The monorepo's `quality` branch does not exist until its first Nightly after
tutors-mono-repo#382; until then Main to RC says so, Test signal stays not measured, and the
Tests light reads the harness half only. The `scoreboard` branch's `mutants.jsonl` fills the
weekly mutants check once a weekly self-test is recorded. Forecasts kept before 1.18.0 get a
strip from their `report.json`, with the monorepo half not measured. To build it locally:
`pnpm harness a3 --site _site --mutants mutants.jsonl` and
`pnpm harness readiness --site _site --mutants mutants.jsonl`.
