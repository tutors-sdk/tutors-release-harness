# Harness 1.34.0 (contract 1.34.0)

Service level objectives: the harness half of tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo#170. The contract
changelog entry is in [../contract.md](../contract.md#changes). A **minor**, for a new check,
artefact name and flag. **The verdict never moves**: the check ships informing.

## Why

Every timing check the harness has is relative: `timing`, `timing-tolerance` and `load` say
"b is slower than a". A candidate as slow as a slow production passes all three, and a production
that misses its own targets is never said to. The monorepo is agreeing service level objectives
(#170), with burn-rate alerts against them in production. The harness should judge a candidate
against the same numbers, before it ships.

## What is new

- **Stricter than the monorepo.** The monorepo's targets are indicative: the college may never want
  "99s". The harness keeps its own floors, `slo/floors.yaml`: 99.9% of requests not 5xx and 99% under
  1s, for every app, on stacks only its own synthetic traffic touches. Each objective is judged at the
  stricter of the two, and the floors alone when the monorepo keeps no file. They are a ratchet:
  `tests/slo.test.ts` pins them, and they are raised by PR, never lowered to make a run pass.
- **Harder on regression.** A candidate significantly worse than production (one-sided
  two-proportion test at 0.05) is a `regression` finding even when the target is met.
- **`slo`** reads `release/slo.yaml` (`--slo`) and judges b against each objective: an app's
  availability (share of responses that are not 5xx) or latency (share of requests at or under a
  threshold that is a bucket bound of `http_request_duration_seconds`). Two sources, each judged on
  its own: the app's own /metrics across the journeys (scope `<id>`), the SLI production's alerts
  read, and for the reader the k6 leg (scope `<id>/load`). A finding ends "(production too)" or
  "(new on b)", as the policy family's do. An objective b meets is an info hunk ending "holds"; one
  that cannot be judged is `.../not-evaluated`.
- **Informing, with no date.** `ENGINE_LEVELS` lists it; the soak watches it. It has no planted
  mutant yet, so it is not eligible to block.
- **Where the file comes from.** `harness release` reads `release/slo.yaml` in the monorepo
  checkout when `--slo` is omitted. `release.yml` and `main-preview.yml` fetch `slo.yaml` beside the
  claims. Until the monorepo commits one, the harness floors are judged alone. A capture recorded
  before 1.34.0 carries no SLI and is not judged.
- The capture keeps the request SLIs per app (`sli` in each metrics snapshot), and `load` gains
  `p99`.

## In the monorepo

`release/slo.yaml` and `docs/slo.md` are tutors-sdk/tutors-mono-repo#170. `pnpm check:release-claims`
must accept `slo` as an artefact name, the same vocabulary change as `timing-tolerance` (#388).

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. New runs gain informing `slo` results from the floors; no verdict moves.
